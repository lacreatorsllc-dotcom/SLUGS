How to pick them
ONE PER KIND OF BOTEach example should teach something different. dca-bot (steady buying), sniper-bot (fast memecoin plays), gold-trader (beyond crypto) and hodl-bot (buy and hold) cover four different ideas. A second sniper or a second maker adds nothing.
FACES THAT READ AT 18–24PXBold, simple pictures work best that small: goobz, brokentyrant, 12g8ge and bagcalls read instantly. meechi's is only 48×48, so it looks soft.
PROMISE ONLY WHAT BAUM CAN DOToday a Baum agent can buy on a schedule (DCA) or copy a trader. sniper-bot, mev-bot, market-maker and grid-bot are fine as name examples, but card 06 says they're bots people can create. Lead with what works today, or say the others are coming.
