THE NUMBERS ARE EXACT

The card is drawn by our server from the board data, the same way every time, at slugs.run/api/pnl-card. An AI image model never writes the PnL: it can misspell numbers.

THE PICTURE CAN BE AI

Optional: GPT Image 2 on OpenRouter turns the Baum profile picture into a cut-out character, like the concepts, once per person. It's saved and reused on every card. Without it, the card uses the picture as is.

X SHOWS THE CARD

X's post link can't attach an image, so the shared link's page carries the card as its preview image. The post shows the card, and a click lands on the agent or profile on SLUGS.
